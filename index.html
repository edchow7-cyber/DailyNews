Himalayan glacier collapse leaves hundreds dead and thousands missing

Rescuers are racing across the Nepal-China border after a glacier collapse triggered a massive mudslide and flooding. Reuters reports at least 750 people are dead and more than 3,000 remain missing, with damaged roads, unstable terrain and new flood threats slowing search efforts. The disaster has also damaged hydropower and other infrastructure, turning the event into both a humanitarian emergency and a major test of climate resilience in the Himalayas.

AI infrastructure increasingly leans on debt financing

Lambda has raised $1 billion in private debt to buy Nvidia chips that it plans to lease to Microsoft, another example of financing structures being built around AI demand. The AI buildout is therefore becoming as much a capital-markets story as a semiconductor story.

California DROP crosses 500,000 users

California says more than 500,000 residents have signed up for its Delete Request and Opt-out Platform, and data brokers have already reported deleting tens of millions of records. Brokers must regularly retrieve and process centralized deletion requests, converting privacy rights into a recurring operational workflow. The milestone shows consumer privacy regulation moving from policy language into measurable, high-volume execution.

Why it matters to Nera: High-volume privacy operations create demand for secure processing and verifiable controls, directly reinforcing Nera’s core value proposition.
