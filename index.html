Isaias weakens to Category 2 but dangerous Gulf landfall remains imminent

Hurricane Isaias weakened to Category 2 Friday evening but remained a dangerous storm as tropical-storm conditions spread across the Alabama–Florida Gulf Coast. The National Hurricane Center still warned of life-threatening surge, destructive winds, flooding rain and tornadoes, with landfall expected overnight near the state line. Alabama received a federal emergency declaration, airports canceled most flights and coastal evacuation orders remained in force. The lower category changes the wind estimate, not the need for residents to stay sheltered and follow local instructions.

California’s CIPA overhaul may end thousands of tracking lawsuits

California changed part of its Invasion of Privacy Act in a way that could extinguish thousands of lawsuits over web-tracking tools. The law removes a private right of action from the pen-register provision often used against analytics and advertising technology. Privacy and labor groups argue that the change weakens a practical enforcement channel at a moment of expanding surveillance. Companies gain litigation relief, but regulators and plaintiffs will test what accountability remains under other statutes.
