Oil shock deepens global bond selloff

Global bond yields pushed to new highs Tuesday as renewed U.S.-Iran fighting lifted oil prices more than 2% and revived inflation fears. Higher energy costs are forcing investors to reconsider how quickly central banks can ease policy, while stocks weakened under the combined pressure of tighter financial conditions and geopolitical risk. The key transmission mechanism remains straightforward: a sustained oil shock can raise inflation, keep borrowing costs elevated and slow investment at the same time.

Private AI moves deeper into enterprise infrastructure

Broadcom’s new VMware Private AI Cloud explicitly pitches enterprise AI as a workload that should be able to remain close to sensitive corporate data instead of forcing that data into public-model infrastructure. The launch is another signal that confidentiality, data residency and governance are moving from niche security requirements into mainstream AI platform positioning. The market is becoming more favorable to vendors that can explain privacy as an enabler of production AI rather than merely as compliance overhead.

Why it matters to Nera: This validates the enterprise buying thesis behind confidential AI, but it also raises the competitive bar: Nera has to show why encrypted computation provides stronger or more flexible guarantees than simply keeping workloads inside a private cloud.

→ Broadcom — VMware Private AI Cloud announcement

Story 2 of 3

Duality ties PETs directly to responsible-AI governance

Duality’s latest responsible-AI guidance argues that privacy-enhancing technologies such as federated learning and fully homomorphic encryption should be part of the technical controls used to satisfy AI governance obligations in regulated industries. The company is positioning PETs not simply as data-security products, but as infrastructure that helps banks, healthcare organizations and governments demonstrate responsible use of sensitive data throughout the AI lifecycle. That framing connects cryptographic capability directly to NIST, EU AI Act and ISO governance conversations that enterprise buyers already understand.

Why it matters to Nera: Duality is increasingly selling PETs through the language of governance and deployability. Nera should continue translating FHE from a technical differentiator into specific finance outcomes that compliance and risk teams can defend.

→ Duality — responsible AI for regulated industries

Story 3 of 3

Zama keeps pairing FHE with regulated financial workflows

Zama’s partnership with Elliptic combines fully homomorphic encryption with compliance screening for confidential financial applications. The commercial logic is increasingly clear: institutions want transaction confidentiality, but they also need monitoring and regulatory controls that survive encryption. Zama’s strategy is therefore moving FHE away from a standalone infrastructure sale and toward concrete workflows such as confidential payments, tokenized assets and regulated on-chain finance.

Why it matters to Nera: The strongest FHE stories continue to attach encryption to a recognizable business process with an obvious compliance need. That reinforces the value of keeping Nera focused on finance workflows rather than selling generic privacy technology.

California advances tougher utility wildfire accountability

California lawmakers are moving forward with a compromise wildfire-liability bill that would increase accountability for utilities whose equipment causes major fires. The proposal includes provisions that could deny executive bonuses after utility-caused disasters while also changing how survivors are paid and how liability is financed. Because PG&E serves much of Northern California, the balance between victim compensation, utility solvency and ratepayer exposure has direct consequences across the Bay Area.

Golden Gate Bridge lane closures begin next week

Two southbound lanes of the Golden Gate Bridge will close during midday hours from September 7 through 10 for pothole filling and asphalt patching. Two lanes will remain open in each direction, but drivers should expect delays between 9:30 a.m. and 2 p.m., with additional maintenance windows planned in October and November. The scheduling avoids the morning commute, but it will still affect daytime travel between Marin and San Francisco.
