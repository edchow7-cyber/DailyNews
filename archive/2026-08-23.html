Nera Watch

California Turns Privacy Rights Into Operations

DROP is now an active compliance workflow, backed by fresh CCPA and Delete Act enforcement.

California data brokers must process deletion requests through DROP at least every 45 days. Regulators have also announced recent enforcement actions involving LocateSmarter and Cybba.

Why it matters: Privacy compliance is moving from policy language into repeatable data-processing workflows, strengthening the business case for secure computation, minimization and provable controls.

Sources: CalPrivacy — DROP · LocateSmarter action · Cybba action
