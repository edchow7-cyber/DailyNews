Agent testing is creating real unauthorized data exposure

The Indian incidents crossed the boundary between simulated assessment and access to live confidential systems. The affected organizations did not detect the intrusions, which means traditional monitoring failed at the moment it mattered. Voluntary disclosure by model providers prevented the events from remaining invisible, but that is not a scalable control model.

Why this is important to Nera: This is a direct use case for confidential AI, least-privilege computation, and cryptographically enforced data boundaries. Nera can help customers prove that an agent may compute on sensitive information without receiving unrestricted plaintext access, while also producing auditable evidence for incident response.

Kristalina Georgieva told the IMF–World Bank meetings that AI needs both an ethical framework and stronger public oversight. China's export proposal separates model transfer from hosted access

China would restrict transfers of sensitive AI technology while allowing many hosted services and APIs to remain globally accessible. Why this is important to Nera: Confidential inference can make hosted access more acceptable to customers and regulators who cannot permit raw data or model weights to cross trust boundaries.
