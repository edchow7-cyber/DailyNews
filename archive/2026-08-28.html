Iran resists new U.S. sanctions as mediators focus on reopening Hormuz

Iran condemned the latest U.S. sanctions and urged other countries not to comply, calling the measures illegitimate while Washington says economic pressure will continue until Tehran changes course. Qatar and Pakistan are trying to restart diplomacy, with the Strait of Hormuz at the center of talks because traffic there remains far below normal even after the U.S. military says it has cleared sea mines. Iran’s security council is considering conditions for reopening the strait, potentially linking access to sanctions relief and compensation. The conflict has now reached the six-month mark with neither side achieving a decisive outcome, making energy flows and diplomatic off-ramps the most consequential near-term variables.

Himalayan flood toll passes 550 with nearly 1,000 still missing

Rescue operations resumed in Nepal and Tibet after concerns about a newly formed lake eased enough for teams to return to the disaster zone. The flood, triggered by a glacier collapse, has killed more than 550 people and left almost 1,000 missing across Nepal and China. More than 90,000 people have been directly affected, and damaged roads, health facilities and communications are complicating the response. The disaster is still evolving, with authorities warning that secondary flooding and disease risks could worsen the humanitarian toll.

Warsh’s Jackson Hole debut becomes the market’s next rate signal

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh is set to deliver his first Jackson Hole address with investors looking for guidance on persistent inflation, bond-market volatility and the path of interest rates. The stakes are unusually high because long-term yields have already moved sharply, raising financing costs for housing, infrastructure and corporate investment. Markets are also watching for evidence that Warsh will preserve Fed independence while balancing slower growth against inflation that has not fully returned to target. His tone could reset expectations for rates even if no immediate policy move is announced.

Judge blocks Pentagon’s Anthropic blacklist

A federal judge blocked the Pentagon from blacklisting Anthropic over the company’s refusal to permit certain uses of its AI in domestic surveillance and autonomous weapons. The court found that the supply-chain-risk designation was unlawful, preventing a move that could have cut Anthropic off from military contracts and damaged its broader reputation. The case is significant because it tests how much leverage government customers can exert over AI companies’ usage restrictions. It also reinforces that model-provider safety policies are becoming commercial and legal issues, not merely internal product choices.

Anthropic proposes a standard for AI agents controlling physical devices

Anthropic has introduced a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard, a framework intended to let AI agents operate programmable physical devices such as microscopes and robotic arms. The goal is to create a common interface between models and scientific or industrial equipment, potentially enabling more autonomous workflows in laboratories and manufacturing. Anthropic says it is working with partners on safety evaluations before opening the framework more broadly. The move expands the agent conversation from software actions into physical-world control, where reliability and safety requirements become substantially higher.

Story 1 of 3

California DROP now has more than 500,000 users

California says more than half a million residents have registered for the Delete Request and Opt-out Platform, which lets consumers send a single deletion request to registered data brokers. Brokers began mandatory processing on August 1 and have already reported deleting tens of millions of records. The scale of adoption shows how quickly privacy rights can become recurring operational workloads once the state creates a centralized mechanism. Compliance is increasingly about provable execution across systems, not just privacy policies.

Why it matters to Nera: High-volume privacy workflows create demand for infrastructure that can match, analyze and act on sensitive data while minimizing exposure. Nera’s value proposition strengthens when customers need useful computation and auditable controls without centralizing or revealing raw information.

→ CalPrivacy — DROP passes 500,000 users

Duality embeds PETs into IBM sovereign-AI infrastructure

Duality Technologies is integrating its privacy-preserving data collaboration technology with IBM Sovereign Core, allowing organizations to analyze distributed sensitive data without exposing the raw information. IBM provides the customer-operated control plane for identity, keys, logs and operations, while Duality supplies PET-enabled collaboration across organizational boundaries. Why it matters to Nera: Duality is validating a go-to-market pattern in which privacy technology is attached to an urgent enterprise outcome. Nera should continue emphasizing confidential finance and analytics workflows rather than asking buyers to purchase FHE as a technology category by itself.

Story 3 of 3

Zama pairs FHE confidentiality with institutional compliance screening

Zama’s partnership with blockchain-intelligence provider Elliptic combines confidential financial applications with wallet-level risk screening. Zama keeps balances and transaction amounts encrypted with fully homomorphic encryption while Elliptic screens addresses for sanctions and other risk signals without seeing the protected financial data. The design is aimed at resolving a core institutional objection to confidential finance: privacy cannot come at the cost of anti-money-laundering and sanctions controls. Zama is therefore competing not just on encryption, but on a full institutional operating model around confidential transactions.

Why it matters to Nera: This is a strong example of PET commercialization through a complete workflow. The competitive lesson is that confidential computation becomes easier to buy when privacy, compliance and business utility are bundled into one operational story.
