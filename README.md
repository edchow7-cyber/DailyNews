# The Outpost — Daily News

A personalized daily news briefing.

## Editorial structure

- **Top Headlines** — the biggest consequential stories of the day, domestic or international.
- **Business** — major business, markets, economic, and corporate developments.
- **Tech** — important technology and AI developments.
- **Nera Watch** — privacy market and regulatory developments plus PETs/privacy-enhancing-technology competitors and adjacent confidential-computing technologies. Include a concise “Why it matters” interpretation when useful.
- **Lakers / NBA** — Lakers-first coverage, with major NBA-wide events included when important.
- **Bay Area** — meaningful Bay Area/local developments; avoid filler.
- **Verse of the Day** — appears above the daily summary.

## Story selection

Each section may contain up to five stories, but there is no quota. Prefer fewer consequential stories over padding a section. The “Today at a glance” summary links to the full sections, and stories link to their original reporting.

## Publishing

`index.html` is always the latest edition. Dated editions are retained under `archive/`.
